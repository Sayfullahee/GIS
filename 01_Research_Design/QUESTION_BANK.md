# RESEARCH DESIGN: MASTER MONITORING QUESTION BANK
## Climate–Hydropower–Erosion–Community Resilience Decision-Support System (CHER-CDSS)
### N-HYPPADEC / Ten Member States of Nigeria
**Document ID:** `RD-QB-2026-v4.1` | **Stage:** 2.2 | **Status:** Approved Baseline  
**Governing Specification:** CHER-CDSS Master Implementation Scaffold v4.1 (October 2026)

---

## 1. Overview & Purpose

This Question Bank operationalizes the CHER-CDSS research agenda into standardized, traceable monitoring questions. Organized across **nine thematic domains**, these questions dictate survey instrument design (XLSForm), spatial analytics algorithms, database queries, and executive reporting tables.

---

## 2. Domain 1: Climate Trends & Extreme Weather Regimes

- **Q1.1:** What are the observed 10-year trends in total annual precipitation, rainy season onset/cessation, and dry spell durations across the ten member states?
- **Q1.2:** Where are extreme rainfall intensity anomalies concentrated (daily rainfall > 50mm, 3-day accumulated > 120mm)?
- **Q1.3:** How do regional temperature trends and heat index anomalies affect baseline evapotranspiration and regional water balance?
- **Q1.4:** To what degree do convective storm tracks correlate with localized flash flood events in plateau and escarpment zones?

---

## 3. Domain 2: Riverine Hydrodynamics & Flood Hazards

- **Q2.1:** What is the recurrence interval and inundation extent of 5-year, 10-year, and 25-year flood events along the River Niger and River Benue corridors?
- **Q2.2:** What proportion of observed flood events are attributable to natural upstream meteorological runoff versus uncoordinated hydropower pulse releases?
- **Q2.3:** What is the maximum flood duration and water depth recorded across sentinel riparian settlements during the peak wet season (August–October)?
- **Q2.4:** Which specific tributary confluences experience the most severe backwater effects during peak mainstream river stages?

---

## 4. Domain 3: Erosion Dynamics & Watershed Geomorphology

- **Q3.1:** What is the spatial distribution, geometric expansion rate, and morphological classification (gully, bank, sheet, rill) of active erosion sites across member states?
- **Q3.2:** What critical infrastructure (roads, bridges, schools, power transmission towers) is situated within the high-risk 50-meter buffer zone of active erosion scarps?
- **Q3.3:** How do land use/land cover changes (deforestation, artisanal mining, peri-urban encroachment) accelerate gully incision rates on the Jos Plateau and Gombe hills?
- **Q3.4:** What is the sediment contribution of active riverbank erosion to reservoir siltation at Kainji, Shiroro, and Dadin Kowa?

---

## 5. Domain 4: Hydropower Operations & Downstream Energy Equity

- **Q5.1:** What are the operational reservoir storage levels, head water elevations, inflow rates, and discharge volumes across Kainji, Jebba, Shiroro, Dadin Kowa, and Kashimbila?
- **Q5.2:** How many days per year do host communities within 10km of hydropower facilities experience complete electric grid service disruptions?
- **Q5.3:** What lead-time warning is provided to downstream communities prior to major emergency spillway openings, and through what communication channels?
- **Q5.4:** What direct physical damages do rapid hydro-pulse releases inflict on downstream artisanal fishing landing sites, aquaculture cages, and irrigation pumps?

---

## 6. Domain 5: Food Security & Agricultural Fragility

- **Q6.1:** What percentage of planted cereal, tuber, and vegetable crop acreage is submerged or destroyed by seasonal flood events per LGA?
- **Q6.2:** How does flood/erosion damage affect household Food Consumption Scores (FCS) and Coping Strategy Index (CSI) ratings 3 to 6 months post-disaster?
- **Q6.3:** What are the measured losses in livestock mortality, grazing access, and artisanal fish catch caused by extreme water events?
- **Q6.4:** How do damaged rural access roads and collapsed culverts affect farm-gate prices and market connectivity during post-harvest marketing?

---

## 7. Domain 6: Community Security & Conflict Impacts (Development Focus)

- **Q7.1:** How do environmental displacements from flood and gully destruction trigger secondary land-use tensions between agrarian communities and pastoralists?
- **Q7.2:** What percentage of surveyed farming households report physical access constraints to their outlying farmlands due to insecurity or washed-out transit corridors?
- **Q7.3:** How do localized security incidents impede the execution, supervision, and maintenance of N-HYPPADEC infrastructure projects?
- **Q7.4:** What safe, aggregated metrics track internally displaced person (IDP) return rates following flood recession and embankment stabilization?

---

## 8. Domain 7: Infrastructure Vulnerability & Service Disruption

- **Q8.1:** What is the physical structural condition (Scale 1–5) and functional operational status of public water boreholes, primary healthcare clinics, and schools in riparian zones?
- **Q8.2:** Which transport culverts and river crossings suffer chronic washouts, isolating communities for more than 7 consecutive days during the wet season?
- **Q8.3:** What is the total financial replacement cost of public and communal assets lost to flood and erosion over the preceding 12-month reporting cycle?
- **Q8.4:** How resilient are communal water supply points against microbiological contamination during high-water flood submergence?

---

## 9. Domain 8: Socioeconomic Vulnerability & Community Adaptation

- **Q9.1:** What proportion of riparian households possess formal savings, cooperative membership, or access to emergency relief financing?
- **Q9.2:** What indigenous flood and erosion coping strategies are actively deployed (sandbag levees, mound farming, seasonal evacuation), and what is their survival efficacy?
- **Q9.3:** What percentage of households have received formal disaster risk reduction (DRR) sensitization or flood early warning alerts prior to peak inundation?
- **Q9.4:** How do female-headed households, elderly populations, and persons with disabilities experience compound hazard vulnerability differently from the baseline population?

---

## 10. Domain 9: MRV, Intervention Effectiveness & Residual Risk

- **Q10.1:** What is the quantified Intervention Effectiveness Score (IES) of completed N-HYPPADEC flood protection dykes and gully check dams relative to pre-project risk?
- **Q10.2:** Did any completed intervention suffer structural failure, erosion bypass, or unexpected maladaptation during subsequent flood seasons?
- **Q10.3:** What residual risk remains in communities following capital intervention completion, and what secondary interventions are required?
- **Q10.4:** How cost-effective (Cost per capita protected in NGN) are N-HYPPADEC hard engineering projects compared to nature-based agroforestry and catchment conservation?
